FAQ

Questions about the Basic plan

Can I start without my own domain and get one later?

It's the most common thing. You start with the address we give you and, whenever you want, we switch to your own domain without rebuilding the website.

Is the web address included?

Yes. We give you an address like yourbusiness.page.pe at no cost while your hosting is active. Your own domain (.com) comes from the Professional plan.

What if my website gets a lot of visitors?

We warn you before we get close to the limit and adjust it with you. You won't be left out without knowing.
